1. About the game and its provider

These terms cover Paint Home: Color Maze Puzzle (com.painthome.kotlin), its game content and this website. Gourav Choudhury publishes the game as a trader on Google Play. Paint Home is a free, ad-supported Android puzzle game for personal entertainment. The Privacy Policy explains how the game and website handle data.

2. Teens and adults

The game is intended for teens and adults, excluding children under 13. If local law requires a higher age or parental permission, those requirements apply. Minors should obtain a parent or guardian's permission where required.

3. Game cash and unlocks

Cash is earned inside the game, cannot be transferred or redeemed, and has no real-money value. There are no real-money purchases. A completed level gives 50–99 cash. One optional rewarded ad adds three times the base reward once, for a total of 200–396 cash (4× the base reward). Skipped levels give no cash or house. Speed and swipe count do not increase cash.

The Original Sponge is free. Twenty cosmetic designs can be permanently unlocked with earned game cash at the price shown before purchase. Prices range from 200 to 30K cash. Buying an already owned design does not charge again. Owned designs and the permanently selected sponge carry across levels. Each design has matching wall paint.

4. One-level trials and optional ads

A rewarded ad can unlock one chosen sponge for the current level attempt. Restarting, advancing or closing the app ends the trial and restores the purchased selection. Trials do not permanently unlock designs. Choose a sponge before painting; the painted house keeps its chosen appearance. Rewards are granted when the ad SDK reports the reward earned, not merely when the ad closes. Ads can be unavailable or fail. Normal puzzle play remains available without choosing a rewarded ad.

5. My City and prices

Finishing a level adds one house to inventory. Placing that earned house on an empty city plot is free and consumes one inventory house. The city has 14 × 14 plots. Each placed house has two decoration upgrades, costing 1K cash each (2K total). Houses do not earn passive cash.

K means one thousand: 1K = 1,000; 1.6K = 1,600; 12.5K = 12,500. Compact balance labels can truncate fractions below 10 cash; full balances and exact listed prices govern transactions. Future versions may adjust prices prospectively. Existing owned skins and city upgrades are retained by this update.

6. Compatibility, saves and availability

The game requires a compatible device running Android 7.0 or later. Core puzzles work offline. Ads and ad privacy forms require an internet connection. Saves are stored locally. Uninstalling or clearing storage can remove progress, cash and unlocks. The game has no cloud save or restore service.
