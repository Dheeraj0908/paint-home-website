Gameplay Guide

How to Play

Simple to learn, deliciously satisfying to master in just three quick steps.

01

Swipe to Slide

Swipe Up, Down, Left, or Right. Your sponge glides smoothly in a straight line until it hits an obstacle, window, bush, or outer edge.

02

Cover Every Tile

Color all white brick tiles across every face of the house. Plan your path so no isolated spots remain unpainted.

03

Collect & Expand

Finish the house to trigger celebratory light rays, collect your random 50–99 cash reward, and unlock the next home for your city!

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about levels, cash, ads, and saved data.

There are 1,000 unique levels! Levels 1 through 25 are classic handcrafted puzzles, while levels 26 through 1,000 are solver-verified procedural campaign levels. No puzzle repeats, and every level is checked using completion paths.

Each time you complete a house, you automatically receive a randomized reward between 50 and 99 cash. You can optionally watch a rewarded video ad on the victory screen to increase your total reward to 4×, or 200–396 cash. Cash has no real-money value and is spent in the Sponge Shop or for City upgrades.

Yes! All 1,000 levels, the City builder, and the Sponge shop are fully playable offline. If you are offline, ads will simply be unavailable, but normal gameplay and progression continue seamlessly.

All game progress (completed levels, cash balance, owned sponge skins, city buildings) is stored 100% locally on your device in private app storage. No personal data or accounts are sent to external game servers.

If you choose to skip a tricky level by watching a rewarded ad, you will advance to the next level immediately. However, skipped levels award 0 cash and no building for your city.
