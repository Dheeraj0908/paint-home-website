Privacy Policy

Application: Paint Home: Color Maze Puzzle Package: com.painthome.kotlin Effective Date: November 2, 2026 Target Audience: General Audience / Teens (13+)

1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to Paint Home: Color Maze Puzzle (the "Application" or "Game"), developed and published by the developer ("we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and providing transparency regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you download, install, and play Paint Home.

This Privacy Policy has been prepared to comply with applicable global privacy standards, including the Google Play Developer Distribution Agreement, Google Play Data Safety policies, Google AdMob Program Policies, the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / UK GDPR), and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA / CPRA).

2. Your Game Data & Local Storage (Play Store Data Safety)

Paint Home does not operate a user account system, cloud server, or remote gameplay database. All gameplay information is saved exclusively and locally on your Android device using Android app-private storage.

The information stored locally on your device includes:

  • Level Progress: Current level number (from Level 1 to 1,000) and completed phases.
  • Virtual Game Cash: Your earned balance of in-game cash (rewarded upon level completion).
  • City Progression: Grid positions and upgrade tiers (Tier 0 to Tier 2) of houses placed in "My City".
  • Cosmetic Unlocks: Sponge skins purchased with game cash, active skin preferences, and temporary ad trials.
  • Gameplay Preferences: Sound effects audio toggle (on/off), procedural artwork seed, and skipped levels.

🔒 No Personal Identification: We do NOT collect, transmit, or store your name, email address, physical address, phone number, contacts, financial bank information, precise GPS location, camera feed, microphone audio, or device storage files.

3. Android Permissions & Privacy-First Architecture

The Application requests only the minimal Android permissions necessary to enable network communication for advertising and consent mechanisms:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required to download ad creatives and display privacy consent dialogs.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required by the Google Mobile Ads SDK to verify network availability before requesting ads.

Explicit Permission Removals

To uphold the highest privacy safeguards for our players, the Application's manifest explicitly removes the following advertising and tracking permissions:

  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID (Android Advertising ID) — REMOVED
  • android.permission.ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID — REMOVED
  • android.permission.ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION — REMOVED
  • android.permission.ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS — REMOVED

This ensures the game does not track your advertising identifier across different applications or build profiling dossiers.

4. Audience & Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Paint Home: Color Maze Puzzle is designed and intended for a general audience aged 13 and older (Teens and Adults). The game is not directed to children under 13 years of age (or under 16 in the European Economic Area).

  • We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or process personal information from children under 13.
  • The game does not include chat rooms, multiplayer messaging, public forums, or user-generated content.
  • All advertising requests sent to Google AdMob are tagged with teen-appropriate, conservative ad rating restrictions.

If a parent or legal guardian discovers that their child has provided personal information to any third party through the game, please contact us immediately at our published support email address, and we will assist in resolving the matter.

5. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep Paint Home free for all players, we display third-party advertisements delivered by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK v25.5.0) and manage privacy disclosures through the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP SDK v4.0.0).

Types of Advertisements

  • Gameplay Banner Ads: Adaptive banner ads may appear in an isolated footer bar located below the puzzle board during active gameplay. The banner is positioned to prevent accidental taps and never overlays or obscures the paintable house surface.
  • Interstitials: Occasional full-screen ads may appear naturally upon level completion transitions. These ads strictly respect frequency caps to preserve an enjoyable gameplay rhythm.
  • Rewarded Video Ads (Opt-In): Completely optional video ads that players can choose to watch in exchange for clear, pre-disclosed in-game perks:
    • Doubling the completion cash reward (+50 to +99 extra cash).
    • Skipping a challenging level (note: skipped levels award 0 cash and no house).
    • Unlocking a 1-level temporary trial for any sponge skin in the shop.

Data Processed by Google

Although our application does not gather personal data, Google AdMob and its partners may automatically process technical device data, including IP address (used to infer broad city/country location for regional ad delivery), device hardware specifications, app interaction telemetry, and diagnostic crash metrics to deliver ads, prevent invalid traffic/click fraud, and verify ad impressions.

Google processes all advertising data in accordance with its own privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services
  • Google My Ad Center (Ad Personalization Controls)

6. User Consent & Privacy Controls (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

European Economic Area (EEA), UK & Switzerland (GDPR)

For users located in the EEA, United Kingdom, or Switzerland, consent is obtained in compliance with the IAB Europe Transparency and Consent Framework (TCF) via Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP).

How to Change Your Ad Choices Anytime: You can review or modify your consent settings at any time directly within the game by tapping More Options (⋮) → Privacy → Ad privacy choices.

California Residents (CCPA / CPRA)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act and California Privacy Rights Act:

  • We do NOT sell your personal information.
  • We do NOT share your personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • We do not collect sensitive personal information.
  • California residents have the right to request information regarding data practices, the right to deletion, and the right not to receive discriminatory treatment.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

Because your game progress is stored strictly on your local device storage, your data remains on your device until you choose to:

  1. Uninstall the application from your device.
  2. Clear the app's data in Android Settings → Apps → Paint Home → Storage → Clear Storage / Clear Data.

Because we do not maintain a central database or associate your device with any account, we cannot remotely view, access, or delete your locally saved data. Clearing your app data or uninstalling the game permanently deletes your progress, cash balance, and unlocked skins.

8. In-Game Economy & Virtual Currency

The game features an in-game virtual currency referred to as "Cash". Virtual cash is earned solely through puzzle gameplay and optional rewarded ads.

  • Virtual cash has no real-world monetary value.
  • Virtual cash cannot be exchanged, redeemed, or refunded for real currency, goods, or services.
  • Virtual cash cannot be transferred, sold, or gifted to other players or devices.
  • There are no real-money in-app purchases (IAP) or microtransactions in Paint Home.

9. Security Measures

We take reasonable and industry-standard precautions to safeguard user data. Local save files are stored in sandboxed Android application-private directories inaccessible to other installed applications. All network communications initiated by third-party SDKs (such as AdMob) are encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).
